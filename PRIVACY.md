# iMinistry Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy explains what data the iMinistry app uses and where that data goes.

## Summary

iMinistry does not collect, send, or sell your data. The developer cannot see any data that you enter in the app.

## Data that you enter

You enter your planned and confirmed hours, your monthly goals, and your service year goals. The app keeps this data only on your device, in the app's local storage. The app does not send this data to a server. The developer does not operate a server for the app.

If you delete the app, iOS deletes this data from your device. Your device backups (iCloud or computer) can contain the data, as for all apps. Apple controls those backups under its own privacy policy.

## Data that the app collects

The app does not collect any data. It contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. The app does not ask for an account, your location, your contacts, or access to your photos.

## Sharing a report

You can send your monthly report to other apps. You must tap a button for each of these actions:

- If you share or copy the report, the app gives the report text to the app that you select, or to the clipboard.
- If you send the report to Hourglass, the app opens a link to `app.hourglass-app.com`. The link contains the month, the year, and your hours. If you have credit hours, the link also contains those hours and their remarks. Hourglass receives this data under its own privacy policy.

The app does not send any data to these services by itself.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be in this file, with a new "Last updated" date.
